Buying workflow

How Do I Buy From JoyaGoo?

A practical, rewritten guide for using JoyaGoo-style finds: start with a product link or spreadsheet row, check the details, review warehouse photos, then ship the order only when it makes sense.

1. Start with a product or spreadsheet row

Most shoppers begin with a Taobao, Weidian, 1688, or spreadsheet find. Instead of opening random links one by one, use a curated route first: sneaker finds, hoodie finds, brand finds, or marketplace finds. This helps you compare similar products before you commit to one item.

On this site, the Finds Spreadsheet and Brand pages are built for that first step.

2. Copy the product link into JoyaGoo

When you find a piece you want to inspect, copy the original marketplace link and paste it into JoyaGoo search or product input. JoyaGoo will usually load the product title, photos, price, seller options, color choices, and size options.

Before adding it to cart, slow down and compare the available versions. Similar-looking listings can differ in material, batch, sizing, seller reputation, and included accessories.

3. Check size, color, version, and notes

Do not treat a spreadsheet row as a final purchase decision. Use it as a discovery shortcut. Check the product page for sizing charts, option names, seller notes, and recent review photos where available. For shoes, confirm the exact model and colorway. For clothing, check measurements instead of relying only on S, M, L, or XL labels.

4. Place the domestic order and wait for warehouse QC

After payment, the seller sends the item to the JoyaGoo warehouse. When it arrives, warehouse photos or QC photos help you inspect shape, color, logo placement, stitching, packaging, and obvious flaws. This is the step where you decide whether to keep, exchange, or return the item before international shipping.

5. Estimate shipping before building a haul

International shipping depends on weight, volume, destination, and line choice. A cheap product can become expensive if it is bulky or if you ship only one small item. Many shoppers build a small haul to spread shipping cost across several pieces, but the best choice depends on your budget and risk tolerance.

6. Choose a shipping line and submit the parcel

Once you are satisfied with the QC photos, select a shipping line, confirm the parcel details, and submit the international shipment. Keep your expectations practical: delivery speed, tracking updates, customs handling, and final cost can vary by country and route.

Quick checklist before buying

  1. Compare at least two similar spreadsheet rows or listings.
  2. Check sizing measurements, not only the displayed size label.
  3. Confirm the color, batch, and product option before payment.
  4. Inspect QC photos carefully when the item reaches the warehouse.
  5. Estimate international shipping before adding too many bulky items.
  6. Keep screenshots or notes for products you may need to compare later.
