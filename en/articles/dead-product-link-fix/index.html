How to Fix a Dead Product Link in Your Joyagoo Spreadsheet

A product link that stops opening interrupts your research, but it does not automatically tell you what happened to the listing or an existing order. Start by preserving the original entry from your Joyagoo Spreadsheet, then investigate before choosing another product from Best Joyagoo Finds. JoyaVault provides a replacement research process; it cannot restore a seller's listing or change an order handled by your shopping agent.

Identify what is actually failing

Open the original marketplace URL directly and compare it with the address saved in your notes. Check whether the link is complete and whether it points to a product rather than a search result or category. A copied fragment can be a different problem from a listing that is genuinely unavailable.

Record what you see: a sign-in request, a loading error, a removed-item message, or a listing whose intended variation cannot be selected. These observations call for different next steps. Avoid labeling every failed attempt “sold out” when the evidence only shows that the page did not load.

If the marketplace page opens but the same link does not load in your agent, keep both observations. Ask the agent about the specific URL if you need help. Do not substitute an unrelated link simply because it loads successfully; the replacement must still meet your product requirements.

Dead link investigation checklist

Preserve useful information before altering the row. It may help you identify the intended product, explain a support question, or avoid repeating the same unsuccessful search later.

  • Keep the original URL, item ID where visible, marketplace, and seller name.
  • Record the product name and the exact option you wanted to buy.
  • Save the measurements and features that made the candidate suitable.
  • Note the date checked and the exact error or missing option you observed.
  • Check whether you already have a paid order associated with the link.
  • Mark the row as unresolved or unavailable instead of silently replacing its contents.
  • Search for alternatives only after you understand what information you need to match.
  • Keep the new candidate separate until you have reviewed its differences.

An old image can help you remember the style, but it is not proof of the replacement's specifications. Keep text details and original references alongside it. Otherwise, a visually similar result can inherit assumptions that were true only of the previous listing.

Compare a replacement from the beginning

Return to the relevant category, such as shoes finds or accessories finds, and look for candidates that meet the same practical need. Search using the product name and important features, not just a familiar photo.

Compare seller, variation, measurements, quantity, included pieces, and the current price. Describe each difference explicitly. If the original size chart helped you choose, do not carry that size decision to another listing without checking its measurements. A replacement can look similar while requiring a different choice.

Use the Taobao, Weidian, and 1688 guide if your search moves to another marketplace. Keep one row per candidate until you choose. Then record why the new entry replaced the old one and which checks you repeated before considering it ready.

Protect the order and parcel decisions already made

If you have already paid, inspect the order status and communicate through the relevant order before placing another purchase. A page that no longer opens does not by itself establish whether the item was purchased, dispatched, or cancelled. Avoid creating a duplicate order based on an assumption about the link.

If the replacement changes the product, revise your QC expectations and parcel plan. A different size, shape, or included accessory can change what you want photographed or how you intend to group the item. Use the QC checklist to update those questions.

Follow the Joyagoo buying guide when the new shortlist is ready. Keep the old and new references until any existing order issue is resolved. Your research notes help you explain the situation, while the platform handling the purchase remains the place to confirm actions and outcomes.

FAQ

Does a dead link mean my paid order is cancelled?

No conclusion about the order follows from the link alone. Review the existing order in your agent account and ask about its status before buying again. Keep the order reference separate from the availability of the public product page.

Can I use a replacement with the same product photo?

Treat it as a new candidate. Compare the seller, description, variation, measurements, quantity, and included pieces. Similar images do not establish that the products are interchangeable or that your previous size choice is appropriate for the new listing.

Should I delete the old spreadsheet row?

Keep it marked as replaced or unavailable if it explains your research or relates to an existing order. Add the new URL separately and record why you switched. This preserves context without leaving the old item looking ready to buy.

Can JoyaVault restore the original marketplace listing?

No. JoyaVault publishes discovery pages and guides. The seller or marketplace controls the listing, and your shopping agent handles questions about a related order. Use this process to organize evidence and compare alternatives rather than expect a guaranteed restoration.
