Why updates matter

A saved spreadsheet row records what caught your attention at one moment. It cannot settle every later question about the listing, received item, coupon, or parcel. Rechecking those details helps you avoid carrying an old assumption into a new payment or shipping decision.

  • Product links and marketplace updates. Revisit the original source to confirm that the listing still opens and the size, color, quantity, and price match your notes. Research replacements as new candidates.
  • QC and warehouse decisions. Keep order expectations beside the received-item photos. Settle unclear details before consolidation instead of letting an unresolved item follow the rest of the haul.
  • Shipping and coupons. Review the current parcel quote and applicable discounts together. A low saved product price does not tell you the final delivered cost.
  • Tracking questions. Distinguish seller-to-warehouse delivery from international parcel delivery. Check which stage and reference you are following before deciding what needs attention.

Use the Joyagoo buying guide for the complete sequence and this updates hub for the question that needs your attention now.

How to use updates before a haul

  1. Refresh your shortlist. Open each original product link and note any changed options or unavailable listings. Use the dead link guide before substituting a new seller or product.
  2. Revisit the marketplace route. If you need alternatives, compare similar items in the same category. Save the replacement URL and the exact variation you intend to buy.
  3. Write your QC checklist. Record the size, color, quantity, and visible details you will compare with warehouse photos. Resolve sizing research before ordering.
  4. Review warehouse readiness. Separate items ready for a parcel from those awaiting delivery, clarification, or a return decision. Check the storage information shown for your own order.
  5. Compare the parcel plan. Review packaging needs, estimated weight and volume, available routes, and the costs of one parcel versus separate parcels where relevant.
  6. Apply the cost checklist. Check eligible coupon terms against the actual charge before paying. Keep a record of what you paid and which stage it covers.

Keep the same notes after dispatch

Save your parcel reference alongside the items it contains. For a tracking question, record the latest event, its date, and whether you are looking at domestic delivery or the international parcel. Review the account's delivery details and contact the relevant order or parcel support if you need clarification.

Latest buyer questions

What if a product link is dead?

Reopen the original marketplace URL to check whether the problem is with the saved link or the listing itself. Keep the product name, seller, item ID, and chosen options in your notes. If the listing is unavailable, research a replacement through the same category and compare its details from scratch; a matching image does not establish that it is the same item.

When should I check QC?

Prepare your QC expectations before ordering, then review the photos when your item reaches the warehouse and before including it in a parcel. Compare the received options with your order and raise unclear details while you can still discuss the item with your agent.

How do coupons affect total cost?

A coupon changes the total only when it applies to an eligible charge and is accepted at payment. Check its expiry, minimum spend, exclusions, and the amount actually deducted. Keep product cost, domestic delivery, international shipping, and selected services separate so you can see what remains payable.

Should I split a parcel?

Compare a combined parcel with separate parcels using the quotes and eligible routes available for your items. Consider weight, volume, packaging, timing, and route restrictions. Splitting is not automatically cheaper, and items with unresolved QC issues should stay out of a ready-to-ship parcel.

Which marketplace should I browse first?

Start with the original source of a find you already like. If you have no shortlist, choose a product category first and explore its Taobao, Weidian, or 1688 links. Compare specific listings, options, and quantities rather than assuming one marketplace is always the best choice.
