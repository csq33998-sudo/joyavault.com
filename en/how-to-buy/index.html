01 / Prepare your shortlist

What You Need Before Placing an Order

  • A valid product link, a clear search term, or enough details for a DIY order
  • A clear idea of the category or style before paying anything
  • Enough visual context to compare options before the QC and shipping steps

Whenever possible, start with the product URL.

Before opening checkout, separate what you know from what you still need to check. You may like the look of a pair of shoes while still being unsure about sizing. A bag may suit your style but be smaller than you expect. Write those questions beside the link so they remain part of your decision.

Keep the original listing, your intended option, and a short comparison note together. This gives you something concrete to review at payment and again at the QC stage. Browsing is useful when it helps you make a smaller, clearer shortlist; collecting more links is not a reason to order before the important details make sense.

02 / From link to delivery

The Joyagoo Buying Flow

Use these seven steps as a reading order. Each stage has a different decision: choose an item, confirm its options, review the warehouse evidence, then plan its delivery.

  1. Step 1

    Find Your Product

    Start with the Spreadsheet Guide when your shortlist is still broad. If you already know what you want, open Shoes or Accessories and narrow the choice before moving into an order. Your aim is to leave browsing with a specific item and its original marketplace URL.

    Keep a short note about why you chose it, the option that caught your attention, and anything the listing has not answered. Check whether a card is a live source listing or a labeled example. A placeholder button is not a product link, and a directory image alone is not enough information to place an order.

  2. Step 2

    Paste the Link on Joyagoo

    Open Joyagoo and paste the original Taobao, Weidian, or 1688 product URL into its search field. Compare the loaded item with your saved reference before continuing. Joyagoo also describes keyword or image search and a DIY order option when a listing cannot be retrieved through the normal search flow.

    If the result looks different, return to the original listing instead of assuming it is the same product. Check the title, main image, seller information, and available options. For a DIY order, be specific about the item you want and the details that are still unclear. Ask for clarification before submitting incomplete instructions.

    Official search and order guidance (opens in a new tab)

  3. Step 3

    Select Size, Color & Options

    Choose your size, color, quantity, and any other required options. Use the listing’s measurements rather than relying only on a familiar size label. Leave concise order notes for details that matter, such as the exact option you mean or a measurement you want checked. Review the completed selection before adding the item to your cart or paying.

    A practical note describes something observable. “Please check the selected black color and size label” is easier to act on than “make sure everything is perfect.” For shoes, keep your measurement reference nearby. For accessories, think about dimensions and intended use. Save the final option names so you have a clear reference when the warehouse photos arrive.

  4. Step 4

    Pay and Wait for Warehouse

    Review the payment breakdown on Joyagoo, then pay for the selected goods and applicable delivery from the seller to the warehouse. International parcel shipping is a separate part of the process. Keep your order reference and follow the status in your account while purchasing and domestic delivery take place.

    Do not treat the first payment as a complete delivered total. Keep a simple note of the item cost, domestic delivery, optional services you select, and the later international shipping charge. If an order asks for your confirmation or an additional payment, read the explanation before responding. An item being purchased, dispatched by the seller, or stored at the warehouse represents a different stage.

    Official fee explanation (opens in a new tab) · Order status guide (opens in a new tab)

  5. Step 5

    Review QC Photos

    When the warehouse inspection photos are available, compare them with your saved listing and selected options. Check the visible color, quantity, labels, shape, and construction details that matter to you. Decide whether you are ready to keep the item or whether you need clarification, more detail, or an after-sales request before shipping it onward.

    A useful QC question points to a specific uncertainty: a size label you cannot read, a mark you cannot identify, or a dimension missing from the photos. Joyagoo lists additional detail photos as an optional service. Check its current return conditions promptly if you see a problem; seller eligibility, timing, and packaging can affect your options. Photos cannot establish how something will feel in use.

    Additional photo services (opens in a new tab) · Current return conditions (opens in a new tab)

  6. Step 6

    Submit for Shipping

    Choose the warehouse items you want to send, then review the delivery address, available shipping routes, and packing options. Compare the quote for the parcel you are actually preparing. Confirm the selected contents and packaging instructions, review the shipping payment, and submit the parcel when the details are right.

    Keep the address complete, including any apartment or unit information you need the carrier to use. Consider both protection and parcel size when choosing packaging. Joyagoo publishes different weighing rules for different routes, so a lighter parcel does not always produce the same saving on every line. If you need a firmer packing reference, review the available rehearsal-packing service and its current charge.

    Route weighing rules (opens in a new tab) · Rehearsal packing guidance (opens in a new tab)

  7. Step 7

    Track and Receive

    After dispatch, follow the parcel information provided in your Joyagoo account and keep the tracking number with your order notes. Check any carrier messages that require a response. The published delivery estimate is a reference, and the parcel can pass through several transport and delivery stages before it reaches you.

    When the parcel arrives, compare its contents with the items you submitted. Keep the packaging while you check the order, and record clear photos if something appears missing or damaged. If you need help, give the relevant support team the parcel reference and a concise explanation. A dated record of what you received is more useful than trying to reconstruct the details later.

    Official logistics overview (opens in a new tab)

03 / Make the checks count

Tips for a Better Experience

  • Compare QC photos with product listing before accepting

    Look at the same detail in both sets of images, rather than judging the whole item from one thumbnail. Differences in lighting and camera angle can affect appearance. If a detail is unclear, describe it and ask for a closer view. Resolve the question before treating the item as ready to ship. Read Joyagoo’s photo and quality notes (opens in a new tab).

  • Remove shoe boxes to reduce shipping weight and cost

    Consider this when you do not need the boxes, but compare the available quote before assuming a saving. Ask about suitable protection for the shoes and keep packaging while any return question is unresolved. The final effect depends on the parcel’s packed size, weight, and selected route.

  • Consolidate multiple items into one package

    Combining suitable warehouse items can avoid paying the initial shipping charge for several separate parcels. It is still worth comparing the combined parcel with separate options, especially when the contents need different protection. Check the route’s conditions and the actual packing choice before deciding. See the official consolidation explanation (opens in a new tab).

  • Use the try-on preview to reduce uncertainty before ordering

    Where a browsing service offers a try-on preview, use it to explore styling and proportions. For example, compare how a shape works with the rest of an outfit before adding more options to your list. Treat the preview as a visual aid, not a measurement, a fit guarantee, or evidence of product quality.

  • Keep one record for each item and parcel

    Save the listing URL, selected options, order reference, and the question you want answered at QC. When shipping, add the parcel reference and tracking number. This small habit makes it easier to compare what you intended to buy with what you received, and to explain a problem if you need support.
