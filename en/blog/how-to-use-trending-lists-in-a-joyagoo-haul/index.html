How To Use Trending Lists In A Joyagoo Haul

Trending lists are useful for discovery, but they work best when you turn them into a focused research queue.

Use trends as signals

A trending product is not automatically the best product. Treat popularity as a starting signal, then compare price, seller photos, available sizes, and whether the item fits your actual haul plan.

Separate interest from intent

Make two lists: products you simply like and products you might actually buy. This keeps your Joyagoo cart cleaner and makes QC review easier later.

Connect trends to categories

Group trending finds under sneakers, hoodies, tees, shorts, bags, and accessories. Category grouping helps you avoid buying too many pieces that serve the same outfit purpose.
