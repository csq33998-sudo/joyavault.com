Shipping Route Planning For A Joyagoo Haul

Shipping is where a cheap find can become expensive. Plan the parcel before you finalize the haul.

Estimate weight and volume

Shoes, jackets, and bags can raise shipping cost faster than light shirts or accessories. Look at actual warehouse weight when possible and be careful with bulky packaging.

Choose a line for the parcel, not the product

Shipping line choice depends on destination, parcel size, delivery expectation, and risk tolerance. The best route for one haul may not be best for another.

Keep timing realistic

Tracking updates can move slowly. Build your haul plan with enough time for seller shipping, warehouse processing, parcel submission, and customs handling.
