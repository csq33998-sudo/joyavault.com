Review Notes That Keep Haul Finds Organized

Good notes make a haul easier to review, especially when you are comparing several products across categories.

Track the reason for each find

Do not only save the link. Write why the item is on your list: color, shape, price, brand intent, outfit role, or replacement for a better option.

Separate product notes from QC notes

Product notes belong before purchase. QC notes belong after warehouse photos arrive. Keeping them separate helps you see whether the final item still matches the original reason you saved it.

Use links as routes

Connect each saved product to a category page or spreadsheet route. When you revisit the haul later, you can compare alternatives faster.
