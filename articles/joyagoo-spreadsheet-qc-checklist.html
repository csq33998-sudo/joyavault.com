Joyagoo Spreadsheet QC Photo Checklist

Use this checklist after a Joyagoo Spreadsheet row turns into a warehouse item. The goal is to catch visual, size, and packaging problems before you keep the item or combine it into a haul.

What to check first

Start with the basic match between the Joyagoo Spreadsheet row and the QC photos: product type, color, size, version, logo placement, and obvious damage. A row can look useful during discovery but still fail when the warehouse photos arrive.

QC checklist

  1. Compare the main color against the seller image and your saved spreadsheet note.
  2. Check shape, symmetry, stitching, print placement, and visible glue or loose threads.
  3. Look for size labels, tags, packaging, and measurement photos when sizing matters.
  4. For shoes, inspect toe shape, heel shape, sole color, branding, and pair symmetry.
  5. For clothing, check length, chest width, sleeve length, graphic placement, and fabric texture.
  6. For bags and accessories, check hardware color, strap shape, logo alignment, zipper lines, and boxed packaging.

When to return a spreadsheet find

Return or exchange becomes more reasonable when the QC photos show a clear mismatch with the row you saved, a visible flaw, a wrong size, or an item that no longer fits the haul plan. If the issue is only a small lighting difference, compare with more photos before deciding.
