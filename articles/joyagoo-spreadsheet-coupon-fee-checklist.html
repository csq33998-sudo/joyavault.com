Joyagoo Spreadsheet Coupon and Fee Checklist

Use this checklist when a spreadsheet price looks attractive but the final cost still depends on domestic shipping, service fees, coupons, international shipping, and packaging choices.

Do not judge a find by product price alone

A Joyagoo Spreadsheet row can make a product look inexpensive, but the real decision comes later. Add separate columns or notes for item price, domestic shipping, service cost, coupon expectation, weight estimate, and final international shipping.

Coupon and fee checklist

  1. Record the product price from the seller page.
  2. Check whether domestic shipping is included or added separately.
  3. Leave room for service charges, currency movement, and payment handling.
  4. Treat coupon claims as a discount to verify, not a guaranteed final price.
  5. Review international shipping only after QC and package weight are clearer.

How this helps spreadsheet shoppers

Cleaner cost notes help you compare two similar finds. One row may have a lower product price but higher shipping or worse packaging risk. Another row may cost more upfront but be easier to ship with the rest of the haul.
