Blog strategy

This blog area is built around Joyagoo haul research rather than a loose list of links. Each article connects one practical buying step to spreadsheet discovery, category routes, QC notes, and shipping planning.

How to use these routes

  1. Start with a category or trending list, not a random product link.
  2. Save comparable finds before adding items to a cart.
  3. Use QC and shipping notes before consolidating a haul.
  4. Return to the spreadsheet routes when you need cleaner category context.
