Category Discovery Notes For Joyagoo Haul Planning

Category browsing makes a haul easier to judge because similar products can be compared side by side.

Start with product roles

Before saving links, decide what role each item should play: everyday sneaker, outerwear layer, summer shorts, bag, accessory, or statement piece. This keeps the haul balanced.

Use category routes

Category routes help you compare similar items without jumping between unrelated listings. Check the sneaker, hoodie, shorts, bag, and accessories pages before finalizing a list.

Watch category-specific risks

Shoes need shape and sizing checks. Clothing needs measurements. Bags and jewelry need material, finish, and hardware review. Each category needs a slightly different QC habit.
