Joyagoo Haul Guide Workflow From List To Shipping

Use a simple workflow to turn scattered haul ideas into a cleaner list, better QC decisions, and a more predictable shipping plan.

Start with research, not checkout

A good Joyagoo haul starts before you add anything to a cart. Save product links from spreadsheet rows, category pages, and marketplace lists, then compare them by item type, price, sizing clarity, seller notes, and photo quality.

Build a short list

Keep only the strongest options. A short list should include the product link, expected size, color, reason you like the item, and any concern that needs checking when warehouse photos arrive.

Move from warehouse QC to shipping

After products reach the warehouse, inspect photos before consolidation. If the shape, logo placement, sizing tag, or color looks wrong, solve that issue before paying for international shipping.

Workflow checklist

  1. Collect comparable products from the same category.
  2. Choose one or two best listings instead of many duplicates.
  3. Check QC photos before consolidation.
  4. Estimate weight and volume before selecting a line.
  5. Keep notes for future haul decisions.
