Choosing An Agent Route After Category Research

After comparing categories, choose the shopping path that gives you the clearest product details and QC control.

Let category research narrow the route

If a category has many similar listings, use the route with clearer photos, better option names, and more predictable sizing information. The goal is not to add more links; it is to reduce uncertainty.

Match the route to your risk

Some items need extra QC attention. Sneakers may need shape checks, jackets may need material and fill review, and accessories may need close-up finish photos.

Keep a backup option

If the first product fails QC or goes out of stock, a second researched option can save time without restarting the whole haul.
